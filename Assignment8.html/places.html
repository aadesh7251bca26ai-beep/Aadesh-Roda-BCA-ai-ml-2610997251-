1. Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal in Agra is a famous marble monument and symbol of love.

2. India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial located in New Delhi.

3. Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal in Jaipur is known for its beautiful pink architecture.

4. Varanasi Ghats

Varanasi is an ancient city known for its spiritual traditions and the River Ganges.

5. Kerala Backwaters

Kerala is famous for its peaceful waterways, greenery and houseboats.
